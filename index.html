TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

What the numbers mean.

Sun & shadows

Sun direction is calculated from latitude, longitude, local date and time using a NOAA-style solar-position approximation. Nine points on each panel are checked against the same roof, tree and neighbour geometry you see.

Power & energy

A clear-sky irradiance approximation, roof angle, cell-temperature estimate, 6% fixed DC losses and 96% inverter conversion produce estimated power. Daily energy integrates 15-minute samples. Inverter clipping is included.

Shading impact

Direct sunlight is reduced by the blocked sample fraction. Diffuse and ground-reflected light are retained. Modules are treated independently; string mismatch, bypass diodes and diffuse sky obstruction are not modelled. This is an illustrative energy estimate.

Assumed site

Dimensions and surrounding geometry are photo-based assumptions. Front bearing is 228° southwest. Kuala Lumpur is an example location, not an identified address. Trees are modelled as opaque crowns.

This is a sales concept, not surveyed engineering. Annual yield, bill savings, structural suitability and electrical compliance require further data and professional verification.
